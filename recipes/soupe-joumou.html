Soupe Joumou

Soup · 2 hr · Serves 8

A celebratory Haitian squash soup with beef, vegetables, pasta and aromatics.

About this dish

Soupe joumou is deeply associated with Haitian Independence Day on January 1. Household versions vary, but squash, meat, vegetables and pasta form a substantial celebratory soup.

Ingredients & step-by-step method

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb beef stew meat
  • 2 tbsp epis
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 2 lb calabaza or butternut squash
  • 8 cups water or stock
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 potatoes
  • Small cabbage
  • 1 celery stalk
  • Thyme
  • 1 whole Scotch bonnet
  • 1 cup small pasta

Method

  1. Season beef with epis, lime, salt and pepper; rest at least 20 minutes.
  2. Brown beef, add 6 cups water and simmer covered 45–60 minutes until beginning to tenderize.
  3. Separately simmer squash until soft, then puree with some cooking liquid.
  4. Add squash puree, carrots, potatoes, celery, thyme and whole Scotch bonnet to beef; simmer 20 minutes.
  5. Add cabbage and pasta; simmer 10–12 minutes until tender.
  6. Remove Scotch bonnet before it bursts, adjust thickness and seasoning, and serve hot.
