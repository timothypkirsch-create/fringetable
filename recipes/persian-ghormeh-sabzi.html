Iran / Persia

Persian Ghormeh Sabzi

Main · 3 hrs · 6 servings

A mountain of herbs cooks down until dark and concentrated, then simmers with lamb, beans and dried lime.

Fringe Table adaptation

About this dish

Ghormeh sabzi is a Persian herb stew built around a large quantity of parsley, cilantro, scallion and fenugreek cooked until deeply aromatic, then combined with meat, beans and dried limes. The finished stew should be dark green-brown, tangy and spoon-coating.

Story & history

Why this dish matters

Ghormeh sabzi is a deeply familiar Iranian home dish with substantial household variation in herbs, beans and meat. Dried lime supplies a characteristic tart-bitter note that fresh citrus cannot fully copy.

Herb ratios vary. This version uses kidney beans and lamb with dried limes.

Before you begin

Preparation notes

Dry herbs well

Wet herbs steam instead of fry.

Fry patiently

The herb mixture should darken without burning.

Pierce dried limes

This lets flavor enter gradually.

Cook it

Ingredients & detailed method

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lamb stew meat
  • 1 cup cooked kidney beans
  • 2 cups parsley
  • 1 cup cilantro
  • 1 cup scallions
  • 1/3 cup fenugreek leaves
  • 1 onion
  • 3 dried Persian limes
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 4 cups water
  • Salt
  • 3 tbsp oil

Method

  1. 1.Finely chop herbs and fry in 2 tbsp oil until darkened and fragrant.
  2. 2.Brown onion and lamb in remaining oil with turmeric.
  3. 3.Add herbs, water and pierced dried limes.
  4. 4.Simmer 90 minutes.
  5. 5.Add beans and continue 30–45 minutes.
  6. 6.Checkpoint: lamb should be tender and stew thick, dark and aromatic.
  7. 7.Adjust salt and lime intensity; serve with rice.
