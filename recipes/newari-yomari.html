Newari Yomari

Dessert · 1 hr 10 min · Serves 12 dumplings

Steamed rice-flour dumplings shaped into tapered parcels and filled with a warm jaggery-sesame mixture.

Fringe Table adaptation

About this dish

Yomari are associated especially with Newar communities of the Kathmandu Valley and with Yomari Punhi. Fillings can differ; jaggery with sesame is a classic direction. Shaping improves with practice, so the goal at home is a well-sealed steamed dumpling rather than perfect ceremonial form.

Cook it

Ingredients & method

Ingredients

  • 2 cups fine rice flour
  • 1 1/4–1 1/2 cups very hot water
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1 tsp neutral oil or ghee
  • 3/4 cup grated jaggery or dark brown sugar
  • 3 tbsp toasted sesame seeds, lightly crushed
  • 2 tbsp unsweetened coconut, optional
  • 1/4 tsp ground cardamom

Method

  1. 1. Mix rice flour and salt. Gradually add hot water, stirring, then knead while warm into a soft, smooth dough. Work in the oil.
  2. 2. Melt jaggery with 1–2 tbsp water over low heat. Stir in sesame, coconut and cardamom; cool until thick enough to spoon.
  3. 3. With damp hands, shape a golf-ball-size piece of dough into a cone, then hollow the center with a finger while keeping the walls even.
  4. 4. Spoon in filling, pinch the opening closed and repeat. Keep finished dumplings covered so they do not dry out.
  5. 5. Steam over simmering water 15–18 minutes, until the surface looks slightly glossy and set. Serve warm.
