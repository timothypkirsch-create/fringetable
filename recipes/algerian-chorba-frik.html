Algeria / Maghreb

Algerian Chorba Frik

Soup · 1 hr 20 min · 6 servings

Tomato, lamb and herbs build the broth while frik—cracked green wheat—adds a smoky grain character and gentle body.

Fringe Table adaptation

About this dish

Chorba frik is an Algerian soup made with meat, tomato, herbs and frik, the roasted green wheat known elsewhere as freekeh. It is especially associated with Ramadan tables, where the warm, aromatic soup commonly begins the evening meal.

Story & history

Why this dish matters

Regional Algerian chorbas vary in meat, herbs, chickpeas and grain. Frik distinguishes this version and gives the soup a toasted flavor that ordinary bulgur does not fully match.

Freekeh is the defining grain. Coarse bulgur can substitute but changes the flavor.

Before you begin

Preparation notes

Rinse frik

It can carry fine husk particles.

Brown lamb first

It deepens the broth.

Add grain late

Overcooked frik can become overly soft.

Cook it

Ingredients & detailed method

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lamb, cubed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion
  • 2 celery stalks
  • 3 tomatoes, grated
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup frik/freekeh
  • 6 cups stock
  • 1/2 cup chickpeas, cooked
  • Cilantro and mint
  • Salt

Method

  1. 1.Brown lamb in olive oil.
  2. 2.Add onion and celery; soften 5 minutes.
  3. 3.Add tomato, paste, paprika and cinnamon; cook until concentrated.
  4. 4.Add stock and simmer 35 minutes.
  5. 5.Stir in frik and chickpeas; simmer 15–20 minutes.
  6. 6.Checkpoint: lamb should be tender and frik chewy-tender, with broth still pourable.
  7. 7.Finish with cilantro, mint and salt.
