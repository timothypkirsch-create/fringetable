Afghan Ashak

Main · 1 hr 30 min · Serves 4–6

Tender leek-filled dumplings layered with garlicky yogurt and savory tomato sauce.

Fringe Table adaptation

About this dish

Ashak are Afghan dumplings traditionally filled with gandana, a leek-like allium. This accessible version uses leeks and scallions while keeping the characteristic yogurt-and-tomato finish.

Cook it

Ingredients & step-by-step method

Ingredients

  • 24 dumpling wrappers
  • 2 cups finely sliced leeks
  • 1 cup sliced scallions
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 cup Afghan garlic yogurt
  • 1 cup Afghan tomato sauce
  • Dried mint

Method

  1. Mix leeks, scallions, coriander and salt; squeeze gently to soften.
  2. Place 2 teaspoons filling in each wrapper. Moisten edges, fold and seal firmly.
  3. Bring a wide pot of salted water to a gentle boil.
  4. Cook dumplings in batches 3–5 minutes, until tender and slightly translucent.
  5. Drain well. Spread yogurt on a platter and arrange ashak over it.
  6. Spoon tomato sauce over the dumplings and finish with more yogurt and dried mint.
