Tunisia

Tunisian Lablabi

Main · 40 min · Serves 4

A Tunisian chickpea-and-bread bowl brightened with cumin, lemon, garlic and harissa, with toppings added to taste.

Fringe Table adaptation

About this dish

Lablabi is a Tunisian chickpea dish in which hot, cumin-scented broth is poured over pieces of bread and finished to the eater’s taste. Harissa, lemon, olive oil, egg, tuna and capers are common additions, but combinations vary.

Cook it

Ingredients & method

Ingredients

  • 2 cans (15 oz each) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 4 cups water or light vegetable broth
  • 4 garlic cloves, lightly crushed
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 4 cups torn day-old crusty bread
  • 2–4 tbsp harissa
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges
  • Extra-virgin olive oil
  • Optional toppings: soft-cooked eggs, tuna, capers, chopped parsley

Method

  1. 1. Simmer chickpeas, water, garlic, cumin and salt for 20 minutes so the broth becomes flavorful.
  2. 2. Divide torn bread among deep bowls.
  3. 3. Ladle chickpeas and plenty of hot broth over the bread.
  4. 4. Add harissa, lemon juice and olive oil to taste.
  5. 5. Finish with any desired traditional-style toppings and serve immediately.
