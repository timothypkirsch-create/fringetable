Senegal

Senegalese Thiakry

Dessert · 1 hr + chilling · Serves 6

Tender millet grains folded into sweet cultured dairy with vanilla and nutmeg for a cool Senegalese dessert.

Fringe Table adaptation

About this dish

Thiakry, also spelled dégué in parts of West Africa, pairs cooked millet granules with sweetened cultured dairy. Texture and sweetness differ widely; this version uses millet couscous for accessibility and keeps the dairy loose enough to coat the grains rather than bury them.

Cook it

Ingredients & method

Ingredients

  • 1 cup millet couscous or fine millet granules
  • 1 1/4 cups water
  • Pinch of salt
  • 2 cups plain whole-milk yogurt
  • 1/2 cup evaporated milk or whole milk
  • 1/3 cup sweetened condensed milk, or sugar to taste
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/4 tsp freshly grated nutmeg
  • 2 tbsp raisins, optional

Method

  1. 1. Cook the millet according to package directions with water and a pinch of salt. Fluff thoroughly and cool completely.
  2. 2. Whisk yogurt, milk, condensed milk, vanilla and nutmeg until smooth.
  3. 3. Break up any millet clumps with your fingers or a fork, then fold the grains into the dairy mixture.
  4. 4. Add raisins if using. Chill at least 30 minutes.
  5. 5. Taste just before serving and adjust sweetness or loosen with a splash of milk.
