Sudanese Ful Medames

Main · 25 min · Serves 4

Warm fava beans mashed with cumin, lime and sesame oil, then topped with fresh tomato, onion and salty cheese.

Fringe Table adaptation

About this dish

Ful is eaten across a wide region, and Sudanese presentations commonly turn the warm fava-bean base into a communal, richly garnished meal. Toppings are flexible; sesame oil, lime, tomato, onion and cheese make a particularly satisfying home version.

Cook it

Ingredients & method

Ingredients

  • 2 cans (15 oz each) fava beans, with some liquid reserved
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 2 tbsp sesame oil
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 1 small tomato, diced
  • 1/4 red onion, finely diced
  • 1/3 cup crumbled feta or white cheese
  • 1 small green chile, sliced, optional
  • Flatbread, for serving

Method

  1. 1. Warm the fava beans with 1/2 cup of their liquid in a saucepan for 8–10 minutes.
  2. 2. Mash about half the beans, leaving plenty whole. Season with cumin and salt.
  3. 3. Stir in half the sesame oil and half the lime juice. Transfer to a shallow serving bowl.
  4. 4. Top with tomato, onion, cheese and chile if using.
  5. 5. Finish with remaining sesame oil and lime; serve immediately with flatbread.
