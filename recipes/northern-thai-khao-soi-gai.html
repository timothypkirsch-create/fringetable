Northern Thai Khao Soi Gai

Noodles / Main · 1 hr 20 min · 4 servings

Soft egg noodles in fragrant coconut curry, crowned with crisp noodles and sharpened at the table with pickles, shallot and lime.

Fringe Table adaptation

About this dish

Khao soi is one of Northern Thailand’s signature noodle dishes and is especially associated with Chiang Mai. The familiar northern Thai form combines egg noodles with chicken, beef or pork in a coconut-milk curry broth, then contrasts the soft noodles with a nest of fried crisp noodles and condiments including pickled mustard greens, shallot, lime and chile oil.

Story & history

Why this dish matters

The Tourism Authority of Thailand describes khao soi as a standard and representative Northern Thai curry noodle dish, built from egg noodles and a mild coconut-milk curry with tender meat. The dish also reflects northern Thailand’s long connections with neighboring Myanmar and Muslim trading communities, and historical narratives around its development vary. Rather than choosing one disputed origin story, this page focuses on the Chiang Mai preparation and its characteristic combination of curry broth, two noodle textures and sharp condiments.

Khao soi has regional relatives and multiple origin narratives. This is specifically a Chiang Mai-style chicken version, not the Lao khao soi noodle dish and not a claim to represent every northern Thai community.

Before you begin

Preparation notes

Use real egg noodles

Fresh Chinese-style egg noodles give the characteristic springy texture and can be split between boiled and fried portions.

Fry the curry paste in coconut cream

Letting the thick coconut cream release some oil helps the paste become fragrant instead of tasting raw.

Serve condiments separately

Pickled mustard greens, shallot, lime and chile oil are part of the eating experience and let each bowl be balanced at the table.

Cook it

Ingredients & detailed method

Ingredients

  • 1 lb fresh egg noodles
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil, plus more for frying noodles
  • 1/3 cup northern Thai or red curry paste
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 can (13.5 oz) full-fat coconut milk, thick cream and thin milk separated if possible
  • 2 lb bone-in chicken thighs or drumsticks
  • 3 cups chicken stock
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce, plus more to taste
  • 1 tbsp palm or brown sugar
  • 1 cup chopped pickled mustard greens
  • 2 shallots, thinly sliced
  • 2 limes, quartered
  • Chile oil, for serving
  • Cilantro, optional garnish

Method

  1. 1.Reserve about one quarter of the noodles for frying. Separate the remaining noodles into four portions for boiling later.
  2. 2.Heat 2 tablespoons oil and the thick coconut cream in a pot over medium heat. Cook until bubbling vigorously, then add curry paste, curry powder and turmeric.
  3. 3.Fry 3–5 minutes until intensely fragrant and small beads of colored oil appear around the paste.
  4. 4.Add chicken and turn to coat. Add remaining coconut milk and stock and bring to a gentle simmer.
  5. 5.Add fish sauce and sugar. Simmer 35–45 minutes until chicken is tender and reaches at least 165°F near the bone.
  6. 6.Checkpoint: broth should be rich but pourable, savory and aromatic with coconut supporting rather than muting the curry. Adjust fish sauce or sugar carefully.
  7. 7.Meanwhile fry the reserved noodles in 350°F oil in small nests until crisp and golden; drain well.
  8. 8.Boil the remaining fresh noodles until just tender, drain and divide among bowls.
  9. 9.Ladle chicken and curry broth over the soft noodles and crown each bowl with crisp noodles. Serve pickled mustard greens, shallot, lime and chile oil alongside.
