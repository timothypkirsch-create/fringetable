Ethiopia / Horn of Africa

Ethiopian Chechebsa

Breakfast · 40 min · 4 servings

Fresh skillet bread is torn while hot, then tossed with niter kibbeh and berbere until every piece is glossy and brick-red.

Fringe Table adaptation

About this dish

Chechebsa, also called kita firfir, is an Ethiopian breakfast made from a simple unleavened skillet bread torn into pieces and coated with spiced clarified butter and berbere. The pleasure comes from contrast: some pieces stay soft, others pick up toasted edges, and the spice-fat mixture reaches every surface.

Story & history

Why this dish matters

Chechebsa belongs to a broad Ethiopian family of dishes that transform bread with seasoned fat or sauce. Unlike injera-based firfir, this version starts with fresh wheat flatbread. Breakfast versions may be served with yogurt, honey or tea depending on household and region.

Names, bread formulas and heat levels vary. This recipe uses a practical wheat-flour kita and niter kibbeh.

Before you begin

Preparation notes

Cook the bread first

Chechebsa is best while the kita is still warm and flexible.

Control berbere heat

Blends vary dramatically; start moderate and add more at the table.

Do not drown it

The bread should be coated, not swimming in butter.

Cook it

Ingredients & detailed method

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3/4 cup warm water, approximately
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil for the pan
  • 3 tbsp niter kibbeh or ghee
  • 1 to 2 tbsp berbere
  • Plain yogurt or honey, optional for serving

Method

  1. 1.Mix flour and salt, then add warm water gradually until a soft, nonsticky dough forms. Rest 10 minutes.
  2. 2.Roll into a round about 1/4 inch thick and cook in a lightly oiled skillet over medium heat until browned in spots on both sides and cooked through.
  3. 3.Checkpoint: the bread should bend without raw dough at the center and show toasted freckles.
  4. 4.Tear hot bread into bite-size pieces.
  5. 5.Melt niter kibbeh in the skillet, stir in berbere for 20–30 seconds, then add bread pieces.
  6. 6.Toss over low heat 2–3 minutes until evenly coated and a few edges crisp.
  7. 7.Serve hot, with yogurt or honey alongside if desired.
