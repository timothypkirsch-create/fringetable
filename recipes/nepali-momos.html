Nepali Chicken Momos

Main · 1 hr 20 min · Serves 4

Juicy steamed dumplings filled with seasoned chicken and aromatics.

About this dish

Momos are beloved across Nepal and the Himalayan region. This chicken version uses a juicy seasoned filling, thin wrappers and a bright tomato-chile chutney.

Ingredients & step-by-step method

Ingredients

  • 24 dumpling wrappers
  • 1 lb ground chicken
  • 1 cup chopped cabbage
  • 1/2 cup chopped onion
  • 3 scallions
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp coriander
  • Tomato-chile chutney

Method

  1. Mix chicken, cabbage, onion, scallions, ginger, garlic, soy sauce and spices until slightly tacky.
  2. Place 1 tablespoon filling in a wrapper and moisten the rim lightly.
  3. Pleat or fold around the filling and pinch firmly closed. Keep finished momos covered.
  4. Lightly oil or line a steamer and arrange momos with space between them.
  5. Steam over strongly simmering water 10–12 minutes until wrappers are glossy and chicken reaches 165°F.
  6. Rest 2 minutes and serve immediately with tomato-chile chutney.
