Haiti / Caribbean

Haitian Legim

Main · 2 hrs · 6 servings

Vegetables are cooked until soft enough to mash together into the dense, savory texture that makes Haitian legim distinct from a loose soup.

Fringe Table adaptation

About this dish

Legim is a Haitian vegetable stew whose identity comes from cooking several vegetables until tender and partially mashing them into a thick, unified mixture. Eggplant, cabbage, chayote and leafy greens are common, while beef, crab or other proteins may be included. Epis supplies the aromatic foundation.

Story & history

Why this dish matters

Legim reflects the vegetable-rich side of Haitian home cooking and is commonly served with rice or rice and beans. Household versions change with season, region and available produce, so the exact vegetable list is flexible while the mashed, concentrated texture remains important.

This version uses beef and a common vegetable combination, but Haitian families vary both protein and produce. The texture matters more than an immutable ingredient list.

Before you begin

Preparation notes

Cut vegetables large

They need to soften fully without vanishing before you can control the final texture.

Use epis generously

It provides the aromatic backbone.

Mash, do not purée

Legim should be thick and rustic, not completely smooth.

Cook it

Ingredients & detailed method

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb beef chuck, cubed
  • 1/2 cup Haitian epis
  • 2 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 small eggplant, cubed
  • 1/2 green cabbage, chopped
  • 1 chayote, peeled and cubed
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 3 cups spinach or watercress
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 4 cups water or stock
  • 1 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil

Method

  1. 1.Marinate beef with half the epis, lime juice and salt for at least 30 minutes.
  2. 2.Brown beef in oil, add tomato paste and remaining epis, then add water or stock and simmer 45 minutes.
  3. 3.Add eggplant, cabbage, chayote and carrots; cover and simmer 30–40 minutes until extremely tender.
  4. 4.Add greens for the final 5 minutes.
  5. 5.Remove beef pieces temporarily and mash vegetables with a potato masher.
  6. 6.Checkpoint: the stew should be thick enough to mound on a spoon while still showing vegetable texture.
  7. 7.Return beef, simmer 10 minutes and adjust salt before serving with rice.
