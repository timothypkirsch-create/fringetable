Assam / Northeast India

Assamese Aloo Pitika

Side · 30 min · 4 servings

Warm potatoes carry raw onion, green chile and mustard oil in one of Assam's simplest, most expressive side dishes.

Fringe Table adaptation

About this dish

Aloo pitika is an Assamese mashed potato preparation typically seasoned with mustard oil, onion, green chile and fresh herbs. Unlike buttery mashed potatoes, it is intentionally rustic and aromatic.

Story & history

Why this dish matters

Pitika preparations are part of Assamese home cooking and can be made from potato, eggplant or fish. The defining technique is mashing and seasoning simply rather than building a sauce.

Mustard oil is central to the flavor; use culinary mustard oil where legally sold for cooking.

Before you begin

Preparation notes

Mash warm

Warm potatoes absorb mustard oil better.

Keep texture rustic

Do not whip smooth.

Add chile gradually

Fresh green chile can be intense.

Cook it

Ingredients & detailed method

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb potatoes
  • 2 tbsp mustard oil
  • 1/3 cup minced red onion
  • 2 green chiles, minced
  • 2 tbsp cilantro
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp lime juice

Method

  1. 1.Boil potatoes until completely tender.
  2. 2.Peel while warm and mash roughly.
  3. 3.Add mustard oil, onion, chile and salt.
  4. 4.Fold without overworking.
  5. 5.Checkpoint: mixture should remain slightly chunky and smell distinctly mustardy.
  6. 6.Add cilantro and lime juice.
  7. 7.Serve warm or room temperature.
