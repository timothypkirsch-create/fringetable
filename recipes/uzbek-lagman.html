Uzbekistan / Central Asia

Uzbek Lagman

Noodles / Main · 2 hrs · 6 servings

Long noodles meet a wok-like meat and vegetable sauce in one of Central Asia's great hand-pulled noodle traditions.

Fringe Table adaptation

About this dish

Lagman is a Central Asian noodle family associated especially with Uyghur and Uzbek cooking. Uzbek versions often pair long chewy noodles with a saucy mixture of lamb or beef, peppers, tomato, radish and aromatics. Home cooks can use purchased fresh wheat noodles while preserving the structure of noodles plus intensely seasoned topping.

Story & history

Why this dish matters

Lagman reflects centuries of exchange across Central Asia, where wheat-noodle traditions moved alongside trade and migration. Hand-pulling the noodles is a specialized craft; this version focuses on the sauce and uses sturdy fresh noodles so the dish remains practical without pretending packaged noodles are identical to a skilled lagman pull.

Uyghur and Uzbek lagman traditions overlap but are not interchangeable. This page uses an Uzbek-style meat-and-vegetable topping and a home shortcut for noodles.

Before you begin

Preparation notes

Use sturdy noodles

Soft pasta disappears under the sauce.

Cut vegetables evenly

Lagman should keep distinct pieces rather than become stew mush.

Cook hot, then simmer

Browning first creates depth before liquid is added.

Cook it

Ingredients & detailed method

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb lamb or beef, cut small
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 daikon or 2 carrots, cut batons
  • 3 tomatoes, chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp cumin seed
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp chile flakes
  • 3 cups stock
  • 1 lb fresh wheat noodles
  • Cilantro, for serving

Method

  1. 1.Brown meat in hot oil in batches until deeply colored.
  2. 2.Add onion, cumin and garlic; cook until fragrant.
  3. 3.Add peppers, radish or carrot and tomatoes; cook 5 minutes.
  4. 4.Add stock and chile, then simmer 30–40 minutes until meat is tender.
  5. 5.Checkpoint: vegetables should remain distinct and the sauce should be loose enough to coat noodles generously.
  6. 6.Boil noodles until chewy-tender, drain and divide among bowls.
  7. 7.Spoon meat, vegetables and broth over noodles and finish with cilantro.
