Gullah Geechee / Lowcountry

Gullah Red Rice

Side / Main · 50 min · Serves 6

Tomato-rich Lowcountry rice with aromatic vegetables and deep savory flavor.

About this dish

Gullah Geechee red rice is a tomato-rich coastal Lowcountry dish connected to West African rice traditions. The goal is fluffy, deeply seasoned grains rather than wet rice.

Ingredients & step-by-step method

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups long-grain rice
  • 4 slices bacon, optional
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 small bell pepper
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 1/2 cups crushed tomatoes
  • 1 1/2 cups chicken stock
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp thyme
  • Salt, pepper and cayenne

Method

  1. Rinse rice until water is mostly clear, then drain very well.
  2. Cook bacon until crisp; remove and leave about 2 tablespoons fat, or use oil.
  3. Cook onion and bell pepper 5–7 minutes. Add garlic and spices for 30 seconds.
  4. Stir in tomatoes and simmer 3–4 minutes. Add rice and coat every grain.
  5. Add stock, bring to a boil, cover tightly and cook on very low heat 18 minutes.
  6. Remove from heat and rest covered 10 minutes. Fluff gently and fold in bacon if using.
