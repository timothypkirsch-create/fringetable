Himachal Pradesh / India

Himachali Siddu

Bread / Main · 2 hrs · 8 buns

A fermented wheat dough wraps a nutty filling and cooks in steam rather than an oven, producing a dense-soft mountain bread.

Fringe Table adaptation

About this dish

Siddu is associated with Himachal Pradesh and neighboring Himalayan areas. The wheat dough is fermented, filled and steamed; walnut, poppy-seed and lentil fillings are among documented variations.

Story & history

Why this dish matters

Siddu reflects cool-climate Himalayan grain cookery and is often eaten hot with ghee or dal. Shape and filling differ across districts and households.

This version uses a walnut filling and a home steamer.

Before you begin

Preparation notes

Let dough rise fully

Underproofed siddu stays heavy.

Grind filling coarse

It should have texture.

Steam without crowding

Buns expand as they cook.

Cook it

Ingredients & detailed method

Ingredients

  • 3 cups whole-wheat flour
  • 1 tsp yeast
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 1/4 cups warm water
  • 1 cup walnuts
  • 1 tbsp poppy seeds, optional
  • 2 scallions
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 2 tbsp ghee

Method

  1. 1.Mix flour, yeast, salt and water into a soft dough; rise 60 minutes.
  2. 2.Grind walnuts, poppy seeds, scallions and cumin coarsely.
  3. 3.Divide dough into 8 pieces, flatten and fill.
  4. 4.Seal and rest 15 minutes.
  5. 5.Steam 18–22 minutes.
  6. 6.Checkpoint: buns should spring back lightly and show no raw dough at the center.
  7. 7.Brush with ghee and serve hot.
