Fossolia

Side · 45 min · Serves 4

Green beans and carrots simmered with onion, tomato and warm spices.

About this dish

Fossolia is an Ethiopian-style vegetable stew of green beans and carrots cooked with onion, tomato and turmeric. It pairs naturally with injera or rice.

Ingredients & step-by-step method

Ingredients

  • 1 lb green beans
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 large onion
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1/2 tsp ginger
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Cook diced onion in oil 8–10 minutes until soft and lightly golden.
  2. Add garlic, turmeric and ginger; stir 30 seconds.
  3. Add tomatoes and salt; cook 5–7 minutes, pressing tomatoes into a sauce.
  4. Add carrots and 1/2 cup water; cover and simmer 8 minutes.
  5. Add green beans and simmer covered 12–15 minutes until tender but not mushy.
  6. Uncover to reduce excess liquid, then taste for salt and pepper.
