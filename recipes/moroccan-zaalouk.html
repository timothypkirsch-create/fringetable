Morocco / Maghreb

Moroccan Zaalouk

Salad / Side · 50 min · 6 side servings

Eggplant and tomato collapse together into a smoky, garlicky salad that is as comfortable warm as it is at room temperature.

Fringe Table adaptation

About this dish

Zaalouk is a Moroccan cooked salad of eggplant, tomato, garlic, olive oil and warm spices. The vegetables should merge into a coarse mash rather than stay as separate cubes, but the finished texture should still feel rustic rather than puréed.

Story & history

Why this dish matters

Cooked salads are central to Moroccan meal spreads, where several small vegetable preparations may accompany bread and a main dish. Zaalouk varies in whether eggplant is boiled, roasted or fried; this version roasts it for concentrated flavor.

Moroccan cooks use different eggplant methods and spice proportions; this is a roasted-eggplant home version.

Before you begin

Preparation notes

Roast until soft

Firm eggplant will never mash properly.

Reduce tomatoes

Watery tomato makes zaalouk loose.

Finish with olive oil

A final drizzle rounds the acidity.

Cook it

Ingredients & detailed method

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggplants
  • 4 tomatoes, grated or chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp chopped cilantro
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Method

  1. 1.Roast eggplants at 425°F until collapsed and soft, 30–40 minutes.
  2. 2.Scoop flesh and chop roughly.
  3. 3.Cook tomatoes, garlic, olive oil, cumin, paprika and salt until thickened.
  4. 4.Add eggplant and mash with a spoon while simmering 10 minutes.
  5. 5.Checkpoint: mixture should hold a mound with no watery pool.
  6. 6.Stir in cilantro and lemon juice.
  7. 7.Serve warm or room temperature with bread.
