Georgia / Caucasus

Georgian Lobio

Main · 1 hr 35 min · Serves 6

A rustic Georgian bean stew seasoned with coriander, blue fenugreek, herbs and vinegar.

Fringe Table adaptation

About this dish

Lobio means beans in Georgian, and the name covers a wide family of preparations rather than one fixed recipe. This version uses red kidney beans, a partly mashed texture, coriander, blue fenugreek, cilantro and vinegar. Serve it with Georgian mchadi for the classic contrast of soft beans and crisp cornbread.

Story & history

A dish with no single “official” formula

Georgia Travel notes that kidney beans reached Georgia in the 16th century and spread into a remarkable variety of regional lobio styles. Some use tkemali, some ham, some walnuts or herbs, and some are served in dedicated clay pots.

The same official source explicitly says there is no universally approved seasoning formula for lobio. That flexibility is part of the tradition, not a flaw in it.

This recipe is one home-style interpretation. Regional Georgian versions may look and taste quite different.

Before you begin

Preparation notes

Beans must be fully tender

Undercooked beans will never mash into the creamy-rustic texture lobio needs.

Reserve the bean liquid

It is the best way to loosen the stew without washing out its flavor.

Add vinegar at the end

Acidity brightens the beans and herbs; add it gradually after the texture is set.

Cook it

Ingredients & detailed method

Ingredients

  • 1 lb dried red kidney beans, soaked overnight, or 3 cans drained
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 tbsp sunflower or neutral oil
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1/2 tsp blue fenugreek, optional but distinctive
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 cup chopped cilantro
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar, plus more to taste
  • Kosher salt
  • Mchadi, for serving

Method

  1. 1.Cook the beans. Cover soaked beans with fresh water and bay leaves and simmer 60–90 minutes. Checkpoint: a bean should crush easily between two fingers with no chalky center.
  2. 2.Reserve liquid. Scoop out at least 1 cup bean cooking liquid before draining excess.
  3. 3.Cook the onion. Heat oil and cook onion 8–10 minutes until soft and golden.
  4. 4.Add spices. Stir in garlic, coriander, blue fenugreek and pepper for 30 seconds.
  5. 5.Mash selectively. Mash about half the beans, leaving the rest whole. Checkpoint: the mixture should look creamy but still visibly contain beans.
  6. 6.Combine. Stir the onion mixture into the beans and add enough reserved liquid to make a thick stew.
  7. 7.Simmer. Cook 10 minutes over low heat, stirring often so the bottom does not catch.
  8. 8.Set the texture. Add liquid if too stiff or simmer uncovered if watery. It should mound softly on a spoon.
  9. 9.Finish. Fold in cilantro and vinegar and season with salt. Taste for a noticeable but balanced tang.
  10. 10.Serve. Serve warm with mchadi, pickles, fresh herbs or vegetables.
