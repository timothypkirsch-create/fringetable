Gullah Geechee / Lowcountry

Gullah Geechee Okra Soup

Soup / Main · 1 hr 20 min · 6 servings

Okra thickens a tomato-rich broth around rice and smoked meat in a Lowcountry soup with direct links to West African foodways.

Fringe Table adaptation

About this dish

Gullah Geechee okra soup brings together okra, tomato, rice and often smoked meat or seafood. Okra's natural mucilage gives the broth body while rice turns the bowl into a substantial meal.

Story & history

Why this dish matters

Gullah Geechee foodways preserve deep connections to West African rice agriculture, okra and one-pot cooking traditions. Modern family versions vary widely in protein, tomato and rice quantity.

This is a home adaptation grounded in documented Gullah Geechee ingredient patterns, not a claim to represent every family recipe.

Before you begin

Preparation notes

Slice okra evenly

Thin rounds thicken more quickly.

Do not over-rinse rice

Some starch helps body.

Salt after smoked meat

It may already contribute plenty.

Cook it

Ingredients & detailed method

Ingredients

  • 1 lb okra, sliced
  • 1 onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 3 tomatoes, chopped
  • 6 oz smoked turkey or ham
  • 6 cups stock
  • 1/2 cup long-grain rice
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • Black pepper
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. 1.Brown smoked meat lightly in a soup pot.
  2. 2.Add onion and garlic; soften.
  3. 3.Add tomatoes and cook until broken down.
  4. 4.Add stock, thyme and okra; simmer 20 minutes.
  5. 5.Add rice and simmer 18–20 minutes.
  6. 6.Checkpoint: okra should be tender and broth lightly thickened while rice remains distinct.
  7. 7.Adjust seasoning and serve hot.
