Georgia / Caucasus

Georgian Badrijani Nigvzit

Appetizer / Side · 1 hr 10 min · 6 appetizer servings

Silky eggplant wrapped around sharp, herby walnut paste—one of Georgia's classic walnut-and-vegetable combinations.

Fringe Table adaptation

About this dish

Badrijani nigvzit is Georgian eggplant with walnuts: thin slices of eggplant are fried or baked, then filled or spread with seasoned walnut paste. Garlic, coriander and vinegar sharpen the nuts, while pomegranate seeds often provide a fresh, tart garnish. The dish is typically served cool or at room temperature as part of a larger table.

Story & history

Why this dish matters

Georgia's official tourism site describes eggplant with walnuts as a dish made in nearly every region of Georgia and served at supra tables. It belongs to a much larger Georgian tradition of combining vegetables with ground walnuts, herbs and acid—the same pantry logic that appears across pkhali and other cold dishes. Household forms vary from rolled spirals to folded slices and flat layered presentations.

Frying is common, but baking the eggplant is also documented and reduces oil absorption. This home recipe uses a skillet method with restrained oil and a traditional-style walnut filling.

Before you begin

Preparation notes

Slice lengthwise and evenly

About 1/4 inch gives enough structure to roll without staying tough.

Salt before frying

A short salting draws surface moisture from the eggplant and helps it brown more predictably.

Do not turn walnuts into butter

Pulse finely but stop before the nuts release enough oil to become a paste on their own.

Cook it

Ingredients & detailed method

Ingredients

  • 2 medium globe eggplants
  • 1 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/4 cup neutral oil, approximately
  • 1 1/2 cups walnut halves
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1/2 tsp ground blue fenugreek
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne or red chile powder
  • 3 tbsp white wine vinegar, plus more to taste
  • 1/4 cup water, approximately
  • 1/3 cup chopped cilantro
  • Pomegranate seeds, for garnish

Method

  1. 1.Slice eggplants lengthwise about 1/4 inch thick. Sprinkle lightly with salt and rest 20 minutes, then blot dry.
  2. 2.Heat a thin layer of oil in a wide skillet over medium heat. Cook eggplant slices in batches until soft and golden on both sides, adding small amounts of oil as needed. Drain on towels or a rack.
  3. 3.Pulse walnuts, garlic, coriander, blue fenugreek, chile and a pinch of salt until very finely ground.
  4. 4.Add vinegar and enough water to make a thick, spreadable walnut mixture. Fold in cilantro.
  5. 5.Checkpoint: the filling should hold its shape on a spoon but spread without tearing the eggplant. Adjust with water by teaspoons if too stiff.
  6. 6.Spread about 1 tablespoon filling across each cooled eggplant slice and either fold in half or roll loosely.
  7. 7.Arrange on a platter and garnish with pomegranate seeds. Rest at least 20 minutes before serving so walnut, garlic and vinegar meld.
