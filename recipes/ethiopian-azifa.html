Ethiopia / Horn of Africa

Ethiopian Azifa

Salad / Side · 50 min · 6 side servings

Lentils move out of the stew pot and into a cool, lemony salad sharpened with mustard, onion and fresh chile.

Fringe Table adaptation

About this dish

Azifa is an Ethiopian lentil salad usually served cool or at room temperature. Green or brown lentils hold their shape, while red onion, chile, lemon or lime and ground mustard create a sharp dressing. Some versions lightly mash part of the lentils for a creamier texture while leaving enough whole for bite.

Story & history

Why this dish matters

Ethiopian cook Eleni Woldeyes describes azifa as a refreshing cold lentil salad often shared at family gatherings and potlucks, with brown mustard seed providing a particularly characteristic flavor. Published Ethiopian recipes differ in ginger, turmeric, tomato and whether onion and chile are raw or briefly sautéed. Its bright acidity makes it especially useful beside richer lentil, meat and vegetable dishes on an injera spread.

Azifa formulas vary. This version keeps the lentils mostly whole and uses mustard, lemon, onion and jalapeño as the central flavoring; households may make it smoother or include additional aromatics.

Before you begin

Preparation notes

Use green or brown lentils

Red lentils collapse too easily for the salad texture used here.

Stop before mushy

Drain as soon as lentils are tender all the way through but still hold their shape.

Dress while barely warm

Slight warmth helps the lentils absorb seasoning, then chilling settles the flavors.

Cook it

Ingredients & detailed method

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups dried green or brown lentils, rinsed
  • 5 cups water
  • 1/2 small red onion, minced
  • 1 jalapeño, seeded and minced
  • 3 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tsp ground mustard
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley, optional

Method

  1. 1.Combine lentils and water in a saucepan, bring to a boil and simmer uncovered 20–30 minutes.
  2. 2.Checkpoint: lentils should be tender at the center but retain distinct skins and shapes. Drain thoroughly.
  3. 3.Whisk lemon juice, olive oil, mustard and salt in a serving bowl.
  4. 4.Add warm lentils and toss. Let cool 10 minutes.
  5. 5.Fold in red onion, jalapeño and parsley if using. Lightly mash 1/4 cup lentils against the bowl if you want a slightly creamier dressing.
  6. 6.Chill 20–30 minutes or serve at room temperature.
  7. 7.Taste again for lemon, mustard and salt before serving with injera or as part of a larger Ethiopian spread.
