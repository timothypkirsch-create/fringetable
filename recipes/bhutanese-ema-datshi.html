Bhutanese Ema Datshi

Main · 25 min · Serves 4

Bhutanese chiles simmered until tender and folded into a simple cheese sauce, served with plenty of rice.

Fringe Table adaptation

About this dish

Ema datshi centers chiles as the vegetable rather than merely a seasoning. Bhutanese cheese differs from common U.S. supermarket cheeses, so this home version uses a mild farmer’s cheese with a little feta for salt and tang. Heat level can be adjusted by mixing hot and mild green chiles.

Cook it

Ingredients & method

Ingredients

  • 10–12 long green chiles, a mix of mild and hot, slit lengthwise
  • 1 small onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 small tomato, chopped, optional
  • 1 1/2 cups water
  • 8 oz farmer’s cheese, crumbled
  • 2 oz feta, crumbled
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • Kosher salt, only if needed
  • Steamed red or white rice, for serving

Method

  1. 1. Combine chiles, onion, garlic, tomato if using and water in a wide pan. Simmer 8–10 minutes until the chiles are tender.
  2. 2. Lower the heat and add farmer’s cheese, feta and butter.
  3. 3. Warm gently without hard boiling, nudging the cheese into the liquid until a loose sauce forms.
  4. 4. Taste before adding salt because the feta may be enough.
  5. 5. Serve immediately with steamed rice.
