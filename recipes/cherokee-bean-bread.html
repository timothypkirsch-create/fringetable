Cherokee Bean Bread

Bread / Side · 45 min · Makes 8

Cornmeal and cooked beans formed into simple, sustaining cakes.

About this dish

Cherokee bean bread combines cornmeal and cooked beans. Family methods vary; this respectful home adaptation forms compact cakes and simmers them gently until firm.

Ingredients & step-by-step method

Ingredients

  • 2 cups fine cornmeal
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 1/2 cups cooked pinto or brown beans
  • 3/4–1 cup hot bean cooking liquid or water
  • Corn husks, optional

Method

  1. Combine cornmeal and salt; fold in drained beans.
  2. Add hot bean liquid gradually until a soft dough holds together. Rest 5 minutes.
  3. With wet hands form 8 compact oval cakes.
  4. If using softened corn husks, wrap each cake loosely.
  5. Lower into gently simmering water and cook 25–30 minutes without a hard boil.
  6. Drain and rest 5–10 minutes; the center should be firm rather than pasty.
