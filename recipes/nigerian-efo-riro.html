Yoruba / Nigeria

Nigerian Efo Riro

Main / Side · 1 hr · 6 servings

A pepper base cooks until its raw edge disappears before greens are folded in just long enough to become tender and glossy.

Fringe Table adaptation

About this dish

Efo riro is a Yoruba vegetable stew built from leafy greens and a pepper-tomato sauce enriched with palm oil and often fish, meat or both. The sauce should be concentrated before the greens enter so the finished dish is rich rather than watery.

Story & history

Why this dish matters

Efo riro is common in Yoruba home cooking and celebration meals, paired with rice or swallows. Greens and proteins vary according to season and household.

This version uses spinach and smoked fish for accessibility; traditional greens and protein choices vary.

Before you begin

Preparation notes

Reduce pepper base

Raw watery sauce ruins the texture.

Add greens late

They should remain green and distinct.

Salt after fish

Smoked fish can be very salty.

Cook it

Ingredients & detailed method

Ingredients

  • 2 lb spinach or amaranth greens
  • 1/3 cup red palm oil
  • 2 red bell peppers
  • 1 Scotch bonnet
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp ground crayfish, optional
  • 8 oz smoked fish
  • 1 tsp salt, as needed

Method

  1. 1.Blend peppers, chile, half the onion and tomatoes coarsely.
  2. 2.Heat palm oil and cook remaining onion until soft.
  3. 3.Add pepper blend and simmer 15–20 minutes until reduced and oil shows at the edges.
  4. 4.Add crayfish and smoked fish and cook 5 minutes.
  5. 5.Fold in chopped greens.
  6. 6.Checkpoint: greens should be tender and glossy with no pool of thin red liquid.
  7. 7.Adjust seasoning and serve with rice or a swallow.
