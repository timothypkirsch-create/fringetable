Horn & Northeast Africa

Ethiopian, Eritrean and Sudanese food traditions include deeply seasoned stews, legumes, vegetables, shared breads and distinctive spice systems. Start with the guides below if you are learning the region, or jump directly into the recipes.

A useful starting path

If you are new to Ethiopian cooking, begin with Shiro Wot because it teaches the flavor relationship between onions, berbere and stew texture with relatively accessible ingredients. Then try Doro Wat to learn the longer onion-reduction technique and the role of niter kibbeh. Serve either with injera.

For a neighboring Eritrean example, Zigni shows how a berbere-driven red stew develops through a long simmer. Sudanese Ful Medames broadens the region further with warm fava beans, sesame oil, lime and fresh toppings.
