Grape Dumplings

Dessert / Heritage · 40 min · Serves 6

Tender dumplings simmered in a vivid, lightly sweet grape broth.

About this dish

Grape dumplings are associated with Cherokee and other Southeastern Indigenous food traditions. Wild grapes are traditional; unsweetened Concord grape juice is used here as an accessible home adaptation.

Ingredients & step-by-step method

Ingredients

  • 4 cups unsweetened Concord grape juice
  • 1/3 cup sugar, or to taste
  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp butter or neutral fat
  • 3/4 cup water

Method

  1. Bring grape juice to a gentle simmer and sweeten only as needed.
  2. Whisk flour, baking powder and salt; cut in butter until crumbly.
  3. Add water gradually and mix just until a soft dough forms.
  4. Roll dough 1/4 inch thick and cut into small strips or squares.
  5. Drop dumplings into simmering juice a few at a time, stirring gently.
  6. Simmer uncovered 12–15 minutes until cooked through and broth thickens slightly. Rest 5 minutes.
