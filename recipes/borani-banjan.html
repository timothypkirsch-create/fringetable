Borani Banjan

Main / Side · 1 hr · Serves 4

Silky eggplant layered with savory tomato and cool garlic yogurt.

About this dish

Borani banjan layers tender Afghan eggplant with tomato and garlic yogurt. This adaptation pan-fries the eggplant in batches so it becomes soft without turning greasy.

Ingredients & step-by-step method

Ingredients

  • 2 medium eggplants
  • Salt
  • Neutral oil
  • 1 onion
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp coriander
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 cup garlic yogurt
  • Dried mint

Method

  1. Slice eggplant into 1/2-inch rounds, salt lightly, rest 20 minutes and blot dry.
  2. Brown eggplant in a thin layer of oil 2–3 minutes per side; drain.
  3. Cook onion until soft. Add garlic, coriander and turmeric for 30 seconds.
  4. Add tomatoes and 1/2 cup water; simmer 10 minutes until thickened.
  5. Nestle eggplant into sauce, cover and simmer 10–12 minutes until completely tender.
  6. Layer garlic yogurt, eggplant and tomato sauce on a platter; finish with dried mint.
