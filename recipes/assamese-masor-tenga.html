Assamese Masor Tenga

Main · 45 min · Serves 4

A clean, tangy fish curry with tomato, turmeric and green chile.

About this dish

Masor tenga is an Assamese fish curry prized for its light sour broth. Tomato is a common souring ingredient, while regional versions may use other sour fruits or greens.

Ingredients & step-by-step method

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb firm white fish
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp mustard or neutral oil
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 cups water
  • 1–2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 green chiles
  • Cilantro

Method

  1. Pat fish dry and season with half the turmeric and salt; rest 10 minutes.
  2. Sear fish lightly in hot oil 1–2 minutes per side, then remove.
  3. Add mustard seeds; when they crackle, add tomatoes and remaining turmeric. Cook until saucy.
  4. Add water and simmer 8–10 minutes.
  5. Return fish and green chiles; simmer gently 5–7 minutes until fish flakes easily.
  6. Turn off heat, add lemon juice gradually, taste for a bright sour finish and garnish with cilantro.
