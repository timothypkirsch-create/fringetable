Harcha

Bread · 35 min · Serves 6

Rustic semolina flatbread with a sandy golden crust and tender center.

About this dish

Harcha is a Moroccan semolina bread cooked on a griddle or skillet and often served warm with butter, honey, cheese or jam.

Ingredients & step-by-step method

Ingredients

  • 2 cups fine semolina, plus more for coating
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp sugar, optional
  • 1/2 cup melted butter
  • About 3/4 cup milk

Method

  1. Mix semolina, baking powder, salt and sugar.
  2. Pour in melted butter and rub through until every grain is coated. Rest 5 minutes.
  3. Add milk gradually until a soft moist dough forms. Do not knead; rest 10 minutes.
  4. Dust the surface with semolina, pat dough 1/2 inch thick and cut rounds.
  5. Heat a dry heavy skillet over medium-low and coat both sides of each round with semolina.
  6. Cook 4–6 minutes per side until golden outside and cooked through. Serve warm.
