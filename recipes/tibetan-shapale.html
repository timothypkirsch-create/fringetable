Tibet / Himalayas

Tibetan Shapale

Dumplings / Main · 1 hr 30 min · 12 pastries

A juicy meat filling is sealed inside sturdy wheat dough and shallow-fried until the seams blister and the crust turns deeply golden.

Fringe Table adaptation

About this dish

Shapale, also written sha phaley, is a Tibetan filled bread or pastry commonly made with seasoned meat enclosed in wheat dough and fried. The filling is intentionally juicy, while the dough must be sturdy enough to contain that moisture without becoming thick and bready.

Story & history

Why this dish matters

Tibetan diaspora restaurants and community cookbooks preserve shapale as both everyday comfort food and a popular restaurant dish. Shapes and fillings vary, and yak meat may be used where available; beef is the practical substitute used here.

This home version uses beef and shallow frying. Regional and household methods vary in filling, shape and frying depth.

Before you begin

Preparation notes

Keep dough firm

Soft dough is harder to seal around a juicy filling.

Chill the filling

Cold filling is easier to portion and reduces leaking.

Seal aggressively

Pinch or crimp the entire edge before frying.

Cook it

Ingredients & detailed method

Ingredients

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup warm water, approximately
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 small onion, minced
  • 3 scallions, chopped
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • Neutral oil for shallow frying

Method

  1. 1.Mix flour, salt and enough water to make a firm smooth dough; rest 30 minutes.
  2. 2.Combine beef, onion, scallion, ginger, garlic, soy sauce and pepper; chill 20 minutes.
  3. 3.Divide dough into 12 pieces and roll each into a 5-inch round.
  4. 4.Place filling on one half, fold over and crimp tightly.
  5. 5.Checkpoint: no filling or juice should touch the sealing edge.
  6. 6.Shallow-fry over medium heat 4–5 minutes per side until deep golden.
  7. 7.Check one pastry: filling should reach 160°F and dough should be cooked through without raw bands.
