Azerbaijan / South Caucasus

Azerbaijani Dovga

Soup · 45 min · Serves 6

A tangy yogurt soup crowded with fresh herbs, rice and chickpeas, equally at home warm or chilled.

Fringe Table adaptation

About this dish

Dovga is one of Azerbaijan's characteristic yogurt soups. Fresh herbs are not garnish here; they are a major ingredient. Rice adds body, chickpeas make the soup substantial, and the yogurt base is carefully heated so it stays smooth rather than separating.

Regional versions vary in the exact mix and amount of greens. Azerbaijan Travel describes Ganja dovga as a yogurt-based soup that may feature seven or more herbs, underscoring how herb-forward the dish can be.

Story & history

A soup defined by fresh herbs and place

Dovga appears across Azerbaijan with local variations, and Ganja is especially associated with an intensely herbal style. The dish reflects a wider South Caucasus tradition of combining cultured dairy with grains, legumes and garden herbs.

Its ability to be served warm, cool or fully chilled also makes it unusually adaptable across seasons. What remains constant is the careful handling of yogurt and the generous quantity of fresh greens.

Herb combinations and proportions differ by family and region. This version uses cilantro, dill, mint and scallion as an accessible home blend.

Before you begin

Preparation notes

Use full-fat yogurt

It is more forgiving under heat and gives dovga a fuller texture.

Stir during the warm-up

The highest curdling risk is while the yogurt mixture moves from cool to hot. Slow heat and frequent stirring matter.

Add herbs late

Long cooking dulls their color and aroma. They need only a few minutes in the hot soup.

Cook it

Ingredients & detailed method

Ingredients

  • 4 cups plain whole-milk yogurt
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 3 cups water or light vegetable stock
  • 1/3 cup long-grain rice, rinsed
  • 1 cup cooked chickpeas
  • 1 packed cup chopped cilantro
  • 1 packed cup chopped dill
  • 1/2 cup chopped mint
  • 4 scallions, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste

Method

  1. 1.Make the yogurt base. Whisk yogurt, egg and flour in a heavy pot until completely smooth with no flour lumps.
  2. 2.Thin gradually. Whisk in the water or stock a little at a time so the mixture remains homogeneous.
  3. 3.Add the rice. Stir in rinsed rice, then place the pot over medium-low heat.
  4. 4.Warm patiently. Stir frequently, scraping the bottom and corners. Checkpoint: steam should rise and the soup should become hot without breaking into curds.
  5. 5.Avoid a hard boil. If vigorous bubbles appear, lower the heat immediately. A gentle tremble is enough to cook the rice.
  6. 6.Check the rice. After about 20 minutes, taste a grain. It should be almost tender with only a slight firm center.
  7. 7.Add chickpeas and scallions. Cook 5 minutes more at a gentle simmer.
  8. 8.Add the herbs. Fold in cilantro, dill and mint. Checkpoint: the soup should look intensely green-flecked and the herbs should smell fresh, not stewed.
  9. 9.Finish briefly. Cook only 2–3 minutes after adding herbs, then season with salt.
  10. 10.Choose the serving temperature. Serve warm immediately, cool to room temperature, or chill thoroughly. If chilled, stir before serving because the rice may thicken the soup.
