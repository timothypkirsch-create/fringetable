Ethiopia / Horn of Africa

Ethiopian Tikil Gomen

Side · 45 min · 6 servings

Cabbage, carrot and potato turn golden with turmeric in a mild Ethiopian vegetable dish designed to balance hotter wots.

Fringe Table adaptation

About this dish

Tikil gomen is an Ethiopian cabbage preparation commonly cooked with carrots and potatoes. Unlike berbere-heavy wots, it is usually mild, with turmeric giving the vegetables a warm yellow color and onion, garlic and ginger supplying depth. The finished vegetables should be thoroughly tender without dissolving into mash.

Story & history

Why this dish matters

Tikil gomen belongs naturally on Ethiopian vegetarian and fasting platters, where mild vegetables help balance lentil and chickpea dishes with more chile or spice. Published Ethiopian and Ethiopian-diaspora versions vary in the use of cumin, ginger, jalapeño and korarima. This adaptation keeps the ingredient list restrained and the texture of each vegetable recognizable.

Household versions differ in spice and vegetable ratios. This is a practical cabbage-carrot-potato form, not a single universal Ethiopian formula.

Before you begin

Preparation notes

Cut vegetables consistently

Even pieces let potatoes and carrots finish close to the same time.

Add cabbage after firmer vegetables

Cabbage softens faster than potato and should not spend the full cook time in the pot.

Keep liquid modest

The vegetables should steam and braise in their own juices, not swim in broth.

Cook it

Ingredients & detailed method

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp olive or neutral oil
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • 3 medium carrots, cut into 1/2-inch pieces
  • 3 medium waxy potatoes, cut into 3/4-inch cubes
  • 1 small green cabbage, cored and cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
  • 1 jalapeño, sliced, optional

Method

  1. 1.Heat oil in a wide covered pot over medium heat. Add onion and cook 6–8 minutes until soft and lightly golden.
  2. 2.Add garlic, ginger and turmeric and stir 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. 3.Add carrots and potatoes, toss in the seasoned oil and cook 4 minutes.
  4. 4.Add water and salt, cover and reduce heat to medium-low. Cook 10 minutes.
  5. 5.Add cabbage and jalapeño if using, folding gently so the cabbage contacts the hot pot. Cover again and cook 12–15 minutes.
  6. 6.Checkpoint: potatoes should be tender when pierced, carrots should retain a little structure and cabbage should be soft without turning to puree.
  7. 7.Uncover and cook 2–3 minutes if free liquid remains. Taste for salt and serve warm with injera and other Ethiopian dishes.
