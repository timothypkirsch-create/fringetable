Eritrea / Horn of Africa

Eritrean Hamli

Side · 35 min · 4 servings

A large pile of greens collapses into a concentrated, glossy side dish seasoned with onion and chile.

Fringe Table adaptation

About this dish

Hamli is an Eritrean leafy-green preparation often made with collards, kale or similar sturdy greens. The method is straightforward: soften onion, add aromatics, then cook the greens until tender enough to scoop easily with injera.

Story & history

Why this dish matters

Greens are an important part of Eritrean and Ethiopian tables, particularly alongside lentil and meat dishes. Household versions differ in chile, tomato and fat. This version keeps the seasoning restrained so the greens remain central.

The word hamli can refer broadly to greens; exact vegetables and seasoning vary by household.

Before you begin

Preparation notes

Remove tough stems

They cook more slowly than leaves.

Do not stop too early

The greens should be tender, not raw-crisp.

Use chile to taste

Heat levels vary widely.

Cook it

Ingredients & detailed method

Ingredients

  • 2 bunches collard greens or kale
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 jalapeño, sliced
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/3 cup water

Method

  1. 1.Wash greens thoroughly, remove thick stems and slice leaves.
  2. 2.Cook onion in oil until soft and lightly golden.
  3. 3.Add garlic and chile for 30 seconds.
  4. 4.Add greens, salt and water; cover 8 minutes.
  5. 5.Uncover and cook 10–15 minutes, stirring.
  6. 6.Checkpoint: leaves should be tender and glossy with little free water.
  7. 7.Adjust salt and serve warm with injera.
