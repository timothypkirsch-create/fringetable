West African Groundnut Stew

Main · 55 min · Serves 6

A savory tomato broth enriched with peanut butter, sweet potato, chickpeas and greens.

About this dish

Groundnut stews appear in many forms across West Africa. This vegetable-forward home adaptation is creamy from peanuts while remaining loose enough to spoon over rice or another grain.

Ingredients & step-by-step method

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 can diced tomatoes
  • 1 large sweet potato
  • 4 cups stock
  • 3/4 cup natural peanut butter
  • 1 can chickpeas
  • 4 cups greens
  • Lime and chopped peanuts

Method

  1. Cook onion in oil 6–8 minutes. Add garlic, ginger and cumin for 30 seconds.
  2. Add tomato paste and cook 1 minute, then stir in tomatoes.
  3. Add sweet potato and stock; simmer 12–15 minutes until nearly tender.
  4. Whisk peanut butter with 1 cup hot broth until smooth, then stir it back into the pot.
  5. Add chickpeas and greens; simmer 8–10 minutes, stirring often, until creamy and tender.
  6. Season with salt and lime, thin with stock if needed, and serve with chopped peanuts.
