Circassian Chicken

Main · 1 hr · Serves 6

Poached chicken dressed with a rich walnut-garlic sauce.

About this dish

Circassian chicken is served cool or at room temperature, with tender shredded chicken coated in a sauce made creamy by walnuts and bread rather than dairy.

Ingredients & step-by-step method

Ingredients

  • 2 lb chicken thighs or breasts
  • 4 cups light stock
  • 1 small onion
  • 2 cups walnuts
  • 2 slices day-old bread
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp coriander
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Poach chicken and onion gently in stock 15–20 minutes until chicken reaches 165°F.
  2. Remove chicken, reserve broth and shred when cool enough to handle.
  3. Pulse walnuts, bread, garlic, paprika and coriander until finely ground.
  4. Add warm broth gradually while blending until the sauce is thick but spoonable.
  5. Fold two-thirds of the sauce through the chicken, adding broth if dry.
  6. Plate, spoon remaining sauce over the top and rest 20 minutes before serving.
