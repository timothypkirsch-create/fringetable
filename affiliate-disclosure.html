Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Fringe Table sometimes links to online retailers to help readers find specialty ingredients that may be difficult to purchase locally. At the moment, the Amazon ingredient links on this site are convenience links and are not configured with affiliate tracking.

When affiliate links are activated

If Fringe Table joins the Amazon Associates program or another affiliate program, qualifying shopping links may earn Fringe Table a commission at no additional cost to the reader. Affiliate-enabled links will be identified clearly and this page will be updated before or when tracking is activated.

Editorial independence

Ingredient recommendations are selected because they are relevant to the recipe, not because a retailer pays to be included. Fringe Table does not guarantee price, availability, quality, dietary suitability or seller performance. Readers should review product details before purchasing.

Amazon Associates wording

If and when the Amazon Associates account becomes active, the required Amazon disclosure language will be added prominently to this page and near eligible links.

Advertising is separate

Affiliate links and Google AdSense advertising are separate monetization methods. Display advertisements are labeled as advertisements and are intentionally kept away from navigation and critical recipe instructions to reduce confusion and accidental clicks.

Questions about affiliate links, product mentions or corrections may be sent to hello@fringetable.com.
