Fringe Table Essential

Puerto Rican Sofrito

A fresh aromatic seasoning base of peppers, onion, garlic and herbs used throughout Puerto Rican cooking.

This is a practical home-kitchen version intended to support the recipes in the Fringe Table collection. Household and regional versions vary, so treat the quantities as a useful starting point rather than a claim to one definitive formula.

Make it

Ingredients & method

Ingredients

  • 1 green bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 cubanelle or mild frying pepper, chopped
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 8 garlic cloves
  • 1 packed cup cilantro
  • 1/2 cup culantro leaves, if available
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Method

  1. 1. Add the peppers, onion, garlic, cilantro and culantro to a food processor.
  2. 2. Pulse until finely chopped but not completely liquefied, scraping the bowl as needed.
  3. 3. Pulse in the olive oil and salt. Refrigerate for several days or freeze in small portions for longer storage.
