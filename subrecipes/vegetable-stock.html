Vegetable Stock

A versatile light stock for soups, stews and sauces.

Ingredients

  • 1 onion, roughly chopped
  • 2 carrots, roughly chopped
  • 2 celery stalks, roughly chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 8 cups water
  • Salt, lightly

Method

  1. Combine everything in a pot and bring to a gentle simmer.
  2. Simmer 45–60 minutes without boiling hard.
  3. Strain and season lightly so the stock remains adaptable.
