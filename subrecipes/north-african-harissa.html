North African Harissa

A chile-forward condiment useful for couscous, stews and grilled foods.

Ingredients

  • 6 dried red chiles, seeded
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1/2 tsp caraway
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and lemon juice

Method

  1. Soften the chiles in hot water, then drain.
  2. Blend chiles, garlic and spices to a paste.
  3. Blend in oil; season with salt and lemon.
