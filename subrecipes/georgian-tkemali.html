Fringe Table Essential

Georgian Tkemali

A tart Georgian plum sauce whose sharp fruitiness cuts through rich meats, beans and stews.

This is a practical home-kitchen version intended to support the recipes in the Fringe Table collection. Household and regional versions vary, so treat the quantities as a useful starting point rather than a claim to one definitive formula.

Make it

Ingredients & method

Ingredients

  • 1 lb tart plums, pitted and chopped
  • 1/3 cup water
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 cup chopped cilantro
  • 2 tbsp chopped dill
  • 1 tbsp chopped mint
  • 1 small green chile, optional
  • 1/2 tsp ground coriander
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. 1. Simmer the plums with water until very soft, 10 to 15 minutes.
  2. 2. Blend or mash until smooth, then return to low heat.
  3. 3. Stir in garlic, herbs, chile if using and coriander. Simmer 3 to 5 minutes; the sauce should remain distinctly tart and spoonable.
  4. 4. Season with salt, cool and refrigerate.
