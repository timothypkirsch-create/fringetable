Fringe Table Essential

Sri Lankan Lunu Miris

A fiery Sri Lankan onion-and-chile relish traditionally served in small amounts beside mild starches such as kiribath.

This is a practical home-kitchen version intended to support the recipes in the Fringe Table collection. Household and regional versions vary, so treat the quantities as a useful starting point rather than a claim to one definitive formula.

Make it

Ingredients & method

Ingredients

  • 1 small red onion, very finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp red chile flakes
  • 1/2 tsp coarse salt
  • 1 tbsp fresh lime juice
  • 1 tbsp finely crushed dried fish or Maldive fish, optional

Method

  1. 1. Pound or firmly mix the onion, chile and salt until the onion softens and releases some juice.
  2. 2. Add lime juice and dried fish if using. Mix until the relish becomes moist and cohesive.
  3. 3. Taste and adjust salt or lime. Serve in small amounts; the relish should be hot, salty and bright.
