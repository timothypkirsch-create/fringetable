Fringe Table Essential

Ethiopian Berbere

A chile-forward Ethiopian and Eritrean spice blend that brings heat, fragrance and depth to wots, tibs and other dishes.

This is a practical home-kitchen version intended to support the recipes in the Fringe Table collection. Household and regional versions vary, so treat the quantities as a useful starting point rather than a claim to one definitive formula.

Make it

Ingredients & method

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tbsp cayenne, more or less to taste
  • 2 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground fenugreek
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ground allspice
  • 1/4 tsp ground cloves
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

Method

  1. 1. Combine all spices in a dry bowl and whisk thoroughly so the cayenne and stronger spices distribute evenly.
  2. 2. Taste a tiny pinch and adjust the cayenne if needed; berbere should be hot, aromatic and slightly sweet rather than only aggressively spicy.
  3. 3. Transfer to an airtight jar and store away from heat and light. For the best aroma, use within about 3 months.
