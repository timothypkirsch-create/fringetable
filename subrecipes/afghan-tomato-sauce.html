Afghan Tomato Sauce

A gently spiced Afghan tomato sauce with onion, garlic and turmeric, designed to spoon over ashak and other dumplings alongside cool garlicky yogurt.

This is a finishing sauce rather than a long-cooked pasta-style tomato sauce. The goal is a bright, savory layer that is thick enough to sit on the dumplings without burying their filling.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 small onion, finely diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 1/2 cups crushed tomatoes
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Cook onion in oil until soft.
  2. Add garlic and turmeric; cook briefly.
  3. Add tomatoes and simmer until thick enough to spoon over dumplings. Season to taste.

How this sauce is used

For Afghan ashak, spoon the warm tomato sauce over the cooked dumplings after adding a layer of garlic yogurt. The contrast between warm tomato, cool yogurt and the herb-filled dumplings is part of the finished dish.

Afghan tomato sauce questions

Is this sauce spicy?

Not necessarily. This version is gently spiced with turmeric and black pepper. Chile can be added to taste, but heat is not required for the sauce to work with ashak.

How thick should it be?

Simmer until a spoon drawn through the pan leaves a brief trail. It should coat a dumpling without running immediately to the bottom of the plate.
