Afghan Garlic Yogurt

A cool garlic-yogurt sauce used to finish Afghan dumpling and vegetable dishes.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups plain whole-milk yogurt
  • 1–2 garlic cloves, finely grated
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • Dried mint, optional

Method

  1. Stir yogurt, garlic and salt until smooth.
  2. Rest 10 minutes so the garlic mellows into the yogurt.
  3. Finish with dried mint if desired and keep chilled until serving.
