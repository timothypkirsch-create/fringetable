Nepali Tomato-Chile Chutney

A bright tomato-and-chile dipping sauce for momos.

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe tomatoes
  • 2 dried or fresh red chiles, to taste
  • 1 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • Salt and lemon juice

Method

  1. Char or roast the tomatoes and chiles until softened.
  2. Blend with sesame, garlic and ginger to a spoonable sauce.
  3. Season with salt and lemon juice.
