Steamed Basmati Rice

A straightforward aromatic rice side for curries and stews.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups basmati rice
  • 2 1/4 cups water
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Rinse rice until the water runs mostly clear.
  2. Combine rice, water and salt; bring to a boil.
  3. Cover, reduce heat to low and cook until tender. Rest covered 10 minutes, then fluff.
