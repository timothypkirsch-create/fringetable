Fringe Table Essential

Vietnamese Nước Chấm

A bright sweet-sour-salty Vietnamese dipping sauce sharpened with lime, garlic and chile.

This is a practical home-kitchen version intended to support the recipes in the Fringe Table collection. Household and regional versions vary, so treat the quantities as a useful starting point rather than a claim to one definitive formula.

Make it

Ingredients & method

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup warm water
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • 1/4 cup fish sauce
  • 3 tbsp fresh lime juice
  • 1 garlic clove, finely minced
  • 1 small red chile, thinly sliced, optional

Method

  1. 1. Stir the warm water and sugar until the sugar dissolves.
  2. 2. Add fish sauce and lime juice, then taste. The sauce should balance salty, tart and sweet without any one element dominating.
  3. 3. Stir in garlic and chile. Let stand 10 minutes before serving so the aromatics perfume the sauce.
