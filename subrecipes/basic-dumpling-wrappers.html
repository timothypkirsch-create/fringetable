Basic Dumpling Wrappers

A simple flour-and-water wrapper dough for home-made dumplings.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3/4 cup warm water, approximately
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Mix flour and salt, then add enough water to form a firm dough.
  2. Knead until smooth, cover and rest 30 minutes.
  3. Roll thin and cut into rounds or squares appropriate for the dumpling you are making.
