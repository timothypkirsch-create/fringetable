Fringe Table Essential

Ghanaian Shito

A deeply savory Ghanaian chile condiment, darkened slowly in oil and served alongside waakye, rice and many everyday meals.

This is a practical home-kitchen version intended to support the recipes in the Fringe Table collection. Household and regional versions vary, so treat the quantities as a useful starting point rather than a claim to one definitive formula.

Make it

Ingredients & method

Ingredients

  • 1 cup neutral oil
  • 1 medium onion, roughly chopped
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp ground dried shrimp or fish, optional
  • 2 to 3 tbsp ground dried chile, to taste
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp ground cloves
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. 1. Blend the onion, ginger and garlic to a coarse paste.
  2. 2. Cook the paste in oil over medium-low heat, stirring often, until moisture cooks away and the mixture begins to darken.
  3. 3. Stir in tomato paste and cook until brick red, then add dried seafood if using, chile, paprika and cloves.
  4. 4. Continue cooking gently until the sauce is dark, glossy and thick. Season with salt and cool before storing.
