Google Analytics and hosting services may process limited technical information such as browser type, device information, IP-derived location, requested pages, referral information, interactions and performance data. These integrations may process limited account or Page identifiers, authorization credentials, publish status and related technical metadata needed to operate the connection. Fringe Table does not use these publishing integrations to sell personal information or to automatically read or respond to private messages.

Access may be revoked through the applicable social platform or by contacting Fringe Table. Operational records related to publishing may be retained as reasonably necessary for security, troubleshooting, duplicate prevention and publication history.
