About Fringe Table

Fringe Table explores recipes from culinary traditions that are often overlooked in mainstream English-language food media.

The idea

Most recipe sites repeatedly circle the same familiar cuisines. Fringe Table looks toward dishes and foodways that carry deep cultural meaning but receive less attention.

Our approach

Recipes are written as practical home-kitchen adaptations. Cultural notes are intended to provide context without pretending there is one definitive version of a living tradition.
