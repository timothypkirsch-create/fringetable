Jollof rice, doro wat, shiro wot, mohinga, Haitian griot, kottu roti, ghormeh sabzi, waakye, momos, mangú, harira, yassa and egusi have meaningful impressions but mostly rank well beyond page one. ## Review cadence

Compare future exports against this baseline. Prioritize pages with at least 10 impressions and average positions 4–20, then pages with high impressions and improving position. Do not rewrite pages solely because they have many impressions at positions 50–90.
