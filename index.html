Why Fringe Table?

Most recipe sites circle the same handful of cuisines.

We look elsewhere — toward dishes that carry deep cultural meaning yet remain underrepresented in English-language cooking media.

Recipes are practical home-kitchen adaptations, while cultural notes point readers toward the communities, traditions and source material behind each dish.
