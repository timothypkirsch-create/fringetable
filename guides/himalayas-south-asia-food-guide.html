Regional guide

Himalayas & South Asia

The current Fringe Table collection spans Nepali and Newari dumplings, Bhutanese chile-and-cheese cooking, Assamese sour fish curry and Sri Lankan coconut-milk rice. These are distinct traditions connected here for exploration, not blended into one generalized cuisine.

Technique threads

Steaming is especially useful to follow through the dumpling recipes, while chile heat plays very different roles in Bhutanese ema datshi and Nepali condiments. Rice shifts from wrapper dough in yomari to a creamy pressed staple in kiribath, and sourness is the defining direction of Assamese masor tenga.
