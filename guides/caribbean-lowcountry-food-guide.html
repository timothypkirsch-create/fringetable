Caribbean & Lowcountry Foodways

Fringe Table currently connects Haitian and Gullah Geechee recipes in this hub because both reveal powerful Atlantic histories of rice, legumes, vegetables, seasoning traditions and adaptation. They remain distinct cuisines with their own histories and should not be collapsed into one generic style.

Follow the rice and seasoning

Rice is a useful thread through this section of the collection, but the surrounding methods matter: Haitian rice and beans draws flavor from beans, herbs, epis and sometimes coconut milk, while Gullah red rice develops its identity through tomato, aromatics and Lowcountry technique. Epis is similarly not a universal Caribbean seasoning but a distinctly Haitian building block.
