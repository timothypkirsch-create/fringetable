Southeast Asia: Myanmar & Laos

The current collection is intentionally focused on Myanmar and Laos. It moves from noodle soups and herb-rich stews to fermented tea-leaf salad, showing how texture, fermentation, fresh aromatics and broth building can matter as much as chile heat.

What to notice

Fermentation is central to lahpet thoke, rice appears as noodles, flour thickener and sticky-rice body, and fresh aromatics such as lemongrass, ginger, galangal, dill and basil are layered rather than treated as garnish. Lao padaek and Myanmar fish sauce traditions also show how fermented savoriness can shape a dish.
