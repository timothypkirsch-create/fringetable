Devzira Rice Substitute for Uzbek Palov

Devzira is valued for the way it absorbs flavor while staying distinct. When it is unavailable, choose rice that can handle layered cooking without becoming mushy.

Best practical options

Long-grain parboiled rice: forgiving and good at staying separate, though its aroma and grain character differ from devzira.

Basmati: accessible and aromatic, but lighter and more slender. Rinse thoroughly, soak briefly, and watch cooking time because it can soften faster.

Medium-grain rice: can work if carefully rinsed and managed, but tends to become stickier and is less forgiving.

Technique matters as much as variety

Whichever rice you choose, rinse until the water is much clearer, soak if appropriate, and avoid aggressive stirring once the rice is layered over the zirvak.
