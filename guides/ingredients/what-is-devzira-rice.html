What Is Devzira Rice?

Devzira is a Central Asian rice associated especially with Uzbek palov, valued for its ability to absorb flavorful cooking liquid while keeping distinct grains.

Why cooks seek it out

Good palov depends on rice that can take on the savory zirvak without collapsing into a soft mass. Devzira is well suited to that job, though exact varieties and processing differ.

How to prepare it

Rinse thoroughly until the water becomes much clearer, then soak before cooking. The point is not to wash away flavor but to remove loose surface starch and hydrate the grain evenly.

If you cannot find it

A sturdy long-grain rice can still make a very good home palov. Treat that as an adaptation rather than claiming it is identical to devzira.
