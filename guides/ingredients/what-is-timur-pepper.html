What Is Timur Pepper?

Timur is a Himalayan spice related to Sichuan pepper, prized for its citrusy aroma and gentle tingling effect.

What it tastes like

Timur is not simply a hotter form of black pepper. It brings bright citrus notes, floral aroma and a light numbing sensation that can sharpen chutneys and savory fillings.

How to use it

A little goes a long way. Toasting briefly and grinding fresh can make the aroma more noticeable; use it in chutneys, spice mixes and dishes where a citrusy pepper note makes sense.
