What Is Lahpet?

Lahpet is fermented tea leaf, a distinctive ingredient in Myanmar cuisine best known internationally through tea leaf salad.

What it tastes like

Prepared lahpet can be savory, tangy, slightly bitter and deeply aromatic. Its intensity is balanced by crisp vegetables, nuts, seeds, fried garlic and crunchy legumes in lahpet thoke.

How it is used

Rather than treating tea only as a drink, Myanmar food traditions include eating fermented tea leaves. The ingredient is often served with contrasting textures so a small amount flavors a much larger bite.
