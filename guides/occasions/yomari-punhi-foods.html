Yomari Punhi Foods

Yomari Punhi is closely associated with Newar communities of the Kathmandu Valley and with the distinctive steamed dumpling called yomari.

The namesake food

Yomari are made from rice-flour dough shaped into tapered parcels and filled, often with a sweet jaggery-based mixture. Their form and cultural context matter as much as the filling.
