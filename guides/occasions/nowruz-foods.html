Nowruz Foods

Nowruz, the Persian New Year, is closely associated with foods that emphasize freshness, herbs and renewal.

Start with kuku sabzi

Fringe Table's current Nowruz anchor is kuku sabzi, a deeply green egg dish in which fresh herbs are the main ingredient rather than a garnish.
