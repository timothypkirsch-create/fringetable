What to Serve With Uzbek Palov

Palov is already a complete, rich rice dish, so the best companions are fresh, acidic and restrained.

Keep the sides simple

A tomato-onion salad, fresh herbs, pickled vegetables or a light yogurt-based accompaniment can cut through the richness without competing with the rice, carrots and meat.
