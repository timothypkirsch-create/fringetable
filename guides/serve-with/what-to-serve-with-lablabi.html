What to Serve With Tunisian Lablabi

Lablabi is designed to be customized at the bowl, so toppings matter more than piling on separate side dishes.

Build the bowl

Start with torn bread and chickpea broth, then adjust with harissa, lemon and olive oil. Soft-cooked eggs, tuna, capers and parsley can make the bowl more substantial while keeping the dish recognizable.
