Regional guide

Caucasus, Central & West Asia

This collection crosses several distinct culinary traditions—Georgia, Azerbaijan, Uzbekistan, Iran/Persia and Circassian foodways. The useful connections are techniques and ingredients such as herbs, beans, rice, walnuts, breads and aromatic spice blends, not the idea of one unified cuisine.

What connects these dishes

Fresh herbs can be structural rather than decorative, walnuts can become the body of a sauce, rice technique can define an entire meal, and region-specific spice blends such as blue-fenugreek-forward Georgian seasonings can carry more identity than a long ingredient list.
