## Required standard for every new recipe

Every recipe added during this expansion must follow the current Fringe Table format and quality bar:

1. No duplicate dish, slug, or substantially duplicate preparation already in the catalog.
2. Specific country, community, or regional framing. Never flatten a broad geographic region into a single cuisine.
3. A clear About This Dish section.
4. A dedicated Story & History section that distinguishes documented history from household or regional variation.
5. At least two credible context/source links when reasonably available, prioritizing primary cultural institutions, respected food publications, museums, universities, government cultural resources, or established culinary historians.
6. Full ingredient list with realistic home-kitchen measurements.
7. Preparation notes before the method when they materially prevent common failures.
8. Detailed numbered method with visual, texture, aroma, temperature, or doneness checkpoints.
9. Practical substitutions only when they preserve the character of the dish; clearly identify when a substitute changes the result.
10. Ingredient sourcing section for genuinely difficult ingredients.
11. Amazon Associates links only for eligible specialty ingredients or culturally/methodologically useful cookware, using tracking ID `fringetable-20`. Never invent ratings, prices, or product claims.
12. Recipe JSON-LD, canonical URL, meta description, Open Graph metadata, and accurate cuisine/category/yield/time fields.
13. Legally reusable image with accurate attribution whenever a suitable image can be verified. If a reliable reusable image cannot be established, do not misattribute an image; use an approved original/generated visual workflow instead.
14. Internal links to relevant regional hubs, guides, ingredient explainers, substitutions, Essentials, collections, or related recipes.
15. Preserve accessibility, responsive design, AdSense separation, newsletter behavior, affiliate disclosures, and existing site architecture.
16. `assets/js/site.js` / `assets/js/site-core.js` remain the catalog source of truth; every new recipe must appear in the catalog, region hub, search, sitemap, and related-discovery system.
17. Do not publish culturally sensitive ceremonial material as a generic home recipe when reliable public culinary documentation does not support doing so. Use explicit home-adaptation language where appropriate.

## Publishing behavior

Until all seven regions reach 20 recipes, expansion publishing should always favor the region with the lowest current recipe count, while rotating enough to avoid publishing an entire region in one block.

After every region reaches 20, resume normal daily publishing at **3 new recipes per day**, balanced across regions and following the same current-format rules above.
